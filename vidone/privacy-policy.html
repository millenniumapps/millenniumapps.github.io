Privacy Policy for VidOne

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the VidOne app for Android (package name: com.millenniumapps.vidone) and iPhone, published by the developer Millenniumapps ("we", "us", or "our"). It explains what information the app accesses, collects, uses, and shares, and the choices you have.

In short

  • VidOne has no accounts and no sign-up. We do not run a server that collects your browsing.
  • Your bookmarks, history, downloads and settings stay on your device.
  • The app uses Google's analytics tools to see how it is used and to fix problems. This is about the app, not about what you browse: the pages you visit, what you search for, your bookmarks, history and downloads are never sent to analytics.
  • The app shows ads on its own screens using Google AdMob. Google may collect some data for that, and asks for your consent where the law requires it.

1. What stays on your device

  • Bookmarks, and the sites you add to the start page.
  • Browsing history, which you can turn off in Settings > Save browsing history, and Continue watching (turn it off in Settings).
  • Files you download, which you can lock behind your fingerprint, face or screen lock.
  • Your settings, and the filter lists used for ad blocking.
  • Website data such as cookies and cached files, kept by your device's web engine like in any browser. Clear it in Settings > Clear cache and Clear cookies and site data.
  • The Debug Console keeps recent page messages and app events in memory only. They are not sent to analytics or anywhere else, and are gone when the app closes.
  • If the app crashes, a short crash report (the error, the time, and the app, phone and system versions) is saved on your device so the Debug Console can show it the next time the app starts. It is not sent to analytics or anywhere else, and Clear in the Debug Console deletes it.

2. Information sent over the network

  • The websites you visit receive the usual information any website receives (your IP address, device and browser details, cookies). What they do with it is covered by their own privacy policies.
  • While you type in the search box, the text you type is sent to Google's search suggestion service to show suggestions. Searches themselves go to the search site you use.
  • The app downloads the EasyList and EasyPrivacy filter lists from easylist.to from time to time. That request carries no information about you beyond what any download does.
  • If you turn on Google SafeSearch in Settings, the app adds a SafeSearch setting to Google search addresses.

3. Analytics

  • The app uses Google's analytics tools (Google Analytics for Firebase) to understand which features are used, to count how many people use the app, and to find and fix errors and crashes.
  • This can include: events such as opening the app or using a feature, the app version, your device model, operating system and language, an identifier for this installation of the app, the advertising identifier where your device allows it, and your approximate location (country or region) worked out from your IP address. Google also receives your IP address when it receives this data.
  • It does not include: the addresses of the pages you visit, the text you search for or type, your bookmarks, your history, your downloads or the content of the websites you open. Your name, email address and phone number are not collected, because the app has no accounts.
  • Google processes this data for us under its own terms. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent before analytics start, together with the consent for ads described below. On iPhone, analytics do not track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • Analytics data is kept for a limited time set in our Google Analytics settings, then deleted.

4. Ads and consent

  • The app uses Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC, to show ads on its own screens. AdMob and Google may collect and use data such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address and general usage to serve and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other places), the app first shows Google's consent form, which covers ads and analytics. On iPhone, the app asks permission before using the advertising identifier for tracking. You can change your choice later in Settings > Privacy choices when it is shown, and in your device settings.
  • Ads from websites you visit are not controlled by the app. The ad blocker only limits them.

5. Permissions

  • Notifications (Android): to show playback controls while a video plays.
  • Local network (Chromecast): to find casting devices on your Wi-Fi.
  • Fingerprint, face or screen lock: to unlock the privacy lock and locked downloads. Biometric data stays in your device's secure system; the app only gets a yes or no.
  • Storage: downloaded files are kept inside the app.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your information. Information goes to others only as described above: the websites you visit, the suggestion service, the filter list host, and Google for ads and analytics. Data sent to Google is sent over an encrypted connection.

7. Your choices

  • Turn off history, Continue watching, or the ad blocker in Settings.
  • Change your consent for ads and analytics in Settings > Privacy choices, where it is shown.
  • Limit or reset the advertising identifier in your device settings: on Android, Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device); on iPhone, Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Clear cache, cookies and site data in Settings.
  • Delete downloads in Downloads.
  • Delete the app to remove everything it stored on the device.

8. Children's Privacy

The app is a general web browser and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, with analytics or otherwise. Websites you open may have their own age rules. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help address it.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not keep any of your data on our servers. Data stored by the app stays in your device's app storage and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. For data held by Google for ads and analytics, see Google's Privacy Policy.

10. Security

Data stored by the app stays in your device's app storage, protected by your device. No system is perfectly secure, and the websites you visit are outside our control.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
