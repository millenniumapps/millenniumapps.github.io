Privacy Policy for Holy Bible KJV

Effective date: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Holy Bible KJV Android app (package name: com.nepalicoder), published on Google Play by the developer Milenniumapps ("we", "us", or "our"). It explains what information the app accesses, collects, uses, and shares, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Holy Bible KJV does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us, such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location. The app has no user accounts and requires no registration.

Bookmarks, highlights, reading progress, and settings are stored locally on your device only. We do not have access to this data. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may automatically collect and process the following information:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information, such as device model, OS version, and app version
  • Ad interaction data, such as ad impressions and clicks
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash logs and performance data

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. How Information Is Used and Shared

Information collected by AdMob is used to show ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and improve ad services. We do not sell, rent, or share any personal information with third parties. Data transmitted to Google is sent over an encrypted connection.

4. Your Choices

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings: Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

5. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help address it.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not keep any of your data on our servers. Data stored locally by the app is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. For data held by Google AdMob, see Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
